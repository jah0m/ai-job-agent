# Japan mid-career interview preparation

Research checked: 2026-09-05. These are public preparation resources, not a
company question leak, statistical frequency ranking, or universal hiring rule.
The short source notes below summarize the sources; the practice prompts and
coaching choices that follow are original adaptations for this workspace.

## Source notes

- [doda: five core interview question groups](https://doda.jp/guide/mensetsu/interview/)
  (updated 2025-07-24): self-introduction, reasons for changing jobs, motivation
  for the employer, relevant strengths/achievements, and questions for the
  interviewer. Recommends concise introductions, truthful and logical career
  explanations, company research, and concrete examples of contribution.
- [Levtech: assessing engineers through technical questions](https://levtech.jp/partner/guide/article/detail/408/)
  (updated 2026-02-12): technical interviews can examine difficult project
  problems, solutions, technical understanding, communication, and problem
  solving. This is employer-facing guidance, not one employer's actual rubric.
- [MHLW: considerations for fair recruitment](https://kouseisaiyou.mhlw.go.jp/consider.html):
  selection should concern job-related aptitude and ability, rather than
  unrelated family background, birthplace, beliefs, or similar private matters.
  Use this as a fairness boundary, not individualized legal advice.

Recheck official employer pages for company-specific claims. Browse again when
asked for current research. Do not reuse advice aimed at new graduates as if
it established mid-career selection criteria.

## Adaptive question menu (original practice prompts)

Choose a small subset for the session; ask questions separately. Follow-ups
below are alternatives, not a bundle to ask in a single turn.

| Focus | Initial question | Possible follow-up | Evidence to listen for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | 今回の職種に関連するご経歴を、簡潔にお話しいただけますか。 | 最も活かせる経験を一つ選ぶと、どれですか。 | Clear scope and relevance, not a full CV recital |
| Career change | 今、転職を考えている理由を教えてください。 | 次の環境で実現したいことは何ですか。 | Honest circumstances and a coherent next step |
| Motivation | 今回の会社・ポジションを選んだ理由を教えてください。 | その期待は、どの公開情報から持ちましたか。 | Employer facts connected to personal goals |
| Contribution | 今回の仕事に近い成果を、一つ説明してください。 | あなた自身が担当した部分はどこですか。 | Individual actions separated from team outcomes |
| Technical decisions | 最近の設計で、判断が難しかった選択を教えてください。 | 採用しなかった案は、なぜ見送りましたか。 | Constraints, alternatives, reasoning, limitations |
| Failure and recovery | 想定どおりに進まなかった仕事について教えてください。 | 次回に向けて、何を変えましたか。 | Ownership, learning, and observed improvement |
| Collaboration | 関係者と意見が分かれた場面を教えてください。 | 判断の基準をどう共有しましたか。 | Listening and a workable decision process |
| Career direction | 次の数年で、どんな役割を担いたいですか。 | このポジションとのつながりをどう考えていますか。 | Realistic aspirations, without invented promises |
| Conditions | 入社時期や働き方で、事前に相談したい条件はありますか。 | 必須条件と相談可能な点を整理できますか。 | Clear boundaries, not unconditional compliance |
| Reverse questions | この仕事について、確認しておきたいことはありますか。 | その回答によって、何を判断したいですか。 | Questions that help the candidate decide fit |

## Coaching choices for this skill

- Try a roughly one-minute introduction, then adapt to the interviewer's request.
  This is rehearsal guidance, not a strict grading threshold.
- Start with the answer, then the reason and one concrete example. For experience
  questions, use situation, responsibility, action, result, and reflection as a
  scaffold, not a memorized speech. For design questions, surface assumptions and
  tradeoffs rather than forcing everything into STAR.
- If metrics are unavailable or confidential, use truthful observable outcomes
  or explain the limitation. Do not reward invented precision.
- Practice the link between career-change reason, company interest, and intended
  contribution. Do not coach someone to deny a bad experience or hide a genuine
  requirement about pay, overtime, location, or remote work.
- For engineering roles, tailor depth to the JD: reliability, security, data,
  testing, delivery, system design, or product tradeoffs only when relevant.
  Admit unknowns and explain how to investigate; do not bluff expertise.
- Offer reverse-question practice about first-quarter expectations, team
  decisions, technical constraints, and success measures. Working conditions
  are legitimate topics too. Never invent a real employer's answer.
- Coach understandable professional Japanese and clarification requests such as
  「ご質問は〇〇という理解でよろしいでしょうか」. Separate language feedback
  from professional competence; do not penalize accent or nationality.
- Do not include unrelated family, religion, birthplace, marriage, or pregnancy
  questions in routine practice. If the user explicitly wants to rehearse an
  inappropriate question they encountered, explain the concern and practice a
  polite boundary or job-related redirection without requiring disclosure.
